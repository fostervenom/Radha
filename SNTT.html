Most Sacred

Chakra Tirtha Kund

Heart of Naimisharanya

The holy circular water body created by Lord Vishnu's Sudarshana Chakra. Taking a dip here washes away all karmic sins.

Open All Day (5 AM - 9 PM) Explore
Shakti Peeth

Maa Lalita Devi Temple

1 Km from Chakra Tirtha

One of the 51 sacred Shakti Peethas. Devotees offer red chunri and prayers to fulfill deep heart desires.

6:00 AM - 8:30 PM Explore
Seat of Puranas

Vyas Gaddi

Near Banyan Tree

The venerable seat under an ancient banyan tree where Sage Ved Vyasa divided the 4 Vedas and compiled 18 Puranas.

6:00 AM - 7:00 PM Explore

Hanuman Garhi & Pandav Kila

High Hill Viewpoint

Houses a massive idol of Lord Hanuman emerging from Patala Loka after rescuing Lord Rama and Lakshmana.

5:30 AM - 8:00 PM Explore

Dashashwamedh Ghat & Gomti

Gomti River Bank

The site where Lord Rama performed the 10th Ashwamedha Yajna. Famous for peaceful evening Gomti Aarti.

Aarti at 6:30 PM Explore

Suta Gaddi & Devdeveshwar

Ancient Assembly Spot

Where Sage Suta Goswami recited the Shrimad Bhagavata Purana to 88,000 assembled sages.
